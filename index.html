Circus Academy

Master the Art of Juggling in 3 Steps

Step 1: The First Throw

Start with one ball. Throw it in a smooth arc to eye level and catch it with the other hand. Practice until you don't have to look at your hands!

Step 2: The X-Exchange

Hold two balls. Throw the first, and when it's at the top, throw the second one underneath it. 1-2, catch-catch!

Step 3: The 3-Ball Cascade

The real show starts now. Keep the rhythm going. Always throw the ball when the previous one is at its peak.

Keep practicing! The circus is waiting for you.

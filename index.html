How to Juggle

Master the art of the 3-ball cascade!

Getting Started

Juggling is all about practice and rhythm. Follow these steps:

Step 1: One Ball

Throw one ball from your right hand to your left hand. The ball should reach eye level and follow an arc shape.

Step 2: The Exchange (Two Balls)

Hold one ball in each hand. Throw the first, and when it reaches its peak, throw the second one underneath it.

Step 3: The Cascade (Three Balls)

This is the classic pattern. Always throw the ball that is "inside" the others. Don't give up!
